Chapter 02

Product overview and operating guide

shop desk · The Good Vets · Proposed multi-tenant online edition

This guide describes the target product, not a claim that a new SaaS is already running. Configuration, legal applicability and permissions determine which controls can be activated. All examples below are training arithmetic, never production seed data.

1. What the software is

shop desk is a browser-based veterinary counter-management system connecting products, batches, purchases, sales, payments, returns, inventory and double-entry books. Different clinics use the same platform but must never share business records, credentials or financial reports accidentally.

A tenant is an independent business workspace. A branch is a location within that tenant. A GST registration identifies the supplier registration used for a transaction; it is not simply the tenant's display name. One tenant may have several branches and registrations, but a new installation can start with one of each. Transfers between registrations are disabled until their tax and document workflow is approved.

The product is not a veterinary diagnosis tool, prescription-authoring system, payment custodian, tax adviser or automatic GST-filing service. A veterinarian/clinic may still have professional recordkeeping obligations in another approved system.

2. Who sees what

Role Typical access Restrictions

Cashier Own drafts/bills, permitted receipts, shared minimal customer/pet directory, own approved return-note reprints No stock corrections, tax-classification changes, cost/margin reports, journals, unrestricted exports or refunds by default.
Inventory operator Products/batches/receipts and quantity-only inventory views if granted Purchase cost, valuation, selling prices and billing totals require their respective permissions; hiding a column alone is insufficient.

Platform operator Tenant lifecycle, plan entitlements, deployment health and incident coordination No default access to clinic invoices or customer data. Exceptional support access must be approved, time-limited and audited.

Names must identify the actual person. Do not share the owner login among cashiers. An owner's membership in one tenant never grants access to another. Staff deactivation must revoke sessions, including already captured sessions used in later requests.

4. Screen-by-screen feature specification

4.1 Overview

Show: selected period, gross billed, customer credits/returns, net billed, confirmed receipts, outstanding balances, refundable amounts, low-stock/expiry alerts and unresolved operational exceptions. Owners may see cost and valuation cards; other users must not receive those values through the API.

Use: choose Today, 7 days, This month, Last month, Financial year, All time or custom From/To. Drill into the contributing source documents. A cashier dashboard is limited to that cashier's authorized work, not the entire clinic's sales.

Manage: every metric needs a definition and a drill-down. Invoice date, receipt date and return date are different measures; do not compare them as if they were the same event.

4.2 Settings and compliance readiness

Show: legal/trade name, address, state/UT, GST status, registration(s), financial-year series, business timezone, receipt methods, invoice templates, permissions, retention rules and readiness issues.

Use: owner enters the actual supplier identity, confirms GST status (regular/composition/unregistered as applicable), obtains classification/document review and selects supported transaction profiles. Configure each series before billing. Store approval evidence and effective dates.

Manage: tax changes create new versions, not changes to old invoice snapshots. Expired licences, unsupported e-invoice obligations, unreviewed tax rules or missing mandatory recipient fields must block the affected sale, not merely display a dismissible warning. “Not required” must be a reviewed applicability conclusion, not a synonym for “not configured”.

4.3 Products and services

Show: name, SKU, barcode, goods/service type, category, UOM, pack conversion, permitted quantity precision, sale price, inclusive/exclusive mode, HSN/SAC, tax treatment/rule, batch requirements, expiry requirements, manufacturer, applicable restrictions and active status. Owner-only fields include purchase cost and margins.

Use: create a product, select verified classifications, set price and UOM, and save. Search by name/SKU/barcode at the counter. A reference lookup can suggest a classification for review; it must not invent a legally approved HSN/rate or silently activate it.

Manage: archive products instead of deleting referenced products. Maintain effective-dated tax/price history. A service has no stock deduction. A product bundle must not receive a guessed “healthcare exempt” or highest-rate mixed-supply treatment; unsupported composite/mixed supplies are blocked pending review.

4.4 Inventory, batches and expiry

Show: physical location, product, manufacturer batch, labelled expiry and precision, available/reserved/non-saleable quantities, supplier/receipt links, status and movement history. Quantity-only views must contain no unauthorized value, cost or margin fields in HTML, JSON, CSV or PDF.

Use: receive stock through Purchases or an approved opening/adjustment workflow. View batches; FEFO chooses eligible first-expiring stock. Near-expiry filters help review 30/60/90-day horizons. A month/year label must retain that original precision and use a reviewed sell-by interpretation.

Manage: block expired, recalled, quarantined or otherwise ineligible batches. Unknown required expiry blocks sale. Physical counting produces an adjustment document with reason and approval; never type over a historical balance. Every movement is append-only, including internal consumption, wastage and damage/expiry write-off with approved accounting/tax-review treatment. Quarantine/disposal is not the same as a sale, customer credit or supplier refund.

4.5 Purchases and goods receipt

Show: supplier, supplier invoice/date/FY, receipt lines, batch/expiry, quantities and bonus units, costs and discounts where permitted, actual supplier GST components, receipt status, payable and payments.

Use: select supplier, enter the genuine supplier bill, record eligible batches and quantities, review costs/tax, then post receipt. The posting adds stock and the accounting liability once. A repeated submission must return the same result, not add stock again.

Manage: duplicate bill checks use the proper supplier/FY scope. Never split a single supplier GST amount into guessed CGST/SGST or mark it automatically claimable ITC. ITC remains pending/ineligible/eligible according to a separately authorized review. A supplier payment reduces the payable, not purchase quantity.

4.6 Supplier returns

Show: original purchase/receipt, original batches, available quantity eligible to send back, reason, dispatch reference, supplier acknowledgment and supplier credit-note status.

Use: select the original receipt, return permitted quantities, approve the inventory movement and record the supplier's actual response. Expired goods returned to a supplier are a documented return/disposal pathway, never a customer sale.

Manage: a buyer's commercial return/debit memo is not automatically the original supplier's statutory GST debit note. Track the genuine supplier credit note or other approved legal treatment separately. Do not reduce GST/ITC just because goods left the shelf. No placeholder supplier-return button may be presented as functional before this flow is implemented and tested.

4.7 Customers, pets and suppliers

Show: only necessary contact/address/GST details, billing and settlement history, and basic pet identifiers. Customer and supplier balances link to the same party ledgers used by accounting.

Use: create/select the party before billing where required. A walk-in anonymous sale is allowed only if that document/transaction legally permits it. Collect mandatory recipient information based on value, GST status, goods restrictions and other reviewed rules.

Manage: do not use phone number as an unquestionable unique identity; several people/pets may share one. Pet records are billing references, not consultations or prescriptions. Avoid free-text human health information and unnecessary identity documents. Corrections to a directory do not rewrite past invoice snapshots.

4.8 Billing and POS

Show: searchable products, eligible quantity/batch/expiry, sale price, tax treatment, discounts, customer, document type, place-of-supply decision, backend-calculated totals and receipt entry. Clearly distinguish a draft, blocked validation, issuing/unknown outcome and an issued document.

Use: create draft → add products → choose customer/context → obtain a backend quote → resolve warnings → finalize once → print the committed document. Default FEFO can be overridden only to another eligible batch by an authorized person. Discount ceilings and price overrides require permission and audit.

Manage: the backend revalidates stock, rules, price permissions and draft version at finalization. A stale quote requires review. Disabling the button helps usability but database idempotency and unique constraints prevent duplicates. Reprinting never posts another sale or stock deduction.

4.9 Invoices, receipts and printing

Show: appropriate document title, verified supplier identity, number/date, required recipient and delivery data, HSN/SAC, quantities/UQC, discounts, taxable values, legally applicable tax components, place of supply, declarations, final total and authentic statutory integration fields where applicable.

Use: select Corporate, Compact or Classic A4; choose 58 mm or 80 mm thermal/POS output; preview/download/reprint. Configure the real printer, paper width, 100% scaling and browser headers/footers. Test in staging or with a non-posting print preview, not a fake production sale.

Manage: narrow receipts must still contain their required particulars, with continuation pages if needed. A payment QR is not an IRP-signed GST QR. “Computer-generated” is not a blanket replacement for all legally required authentication. Preserve original snapshots, template version and original artifact; show current settlement separately and label its as-of time.

4.10 Payments and cash close

Show: invoice, confirmed receipt allocations, pending/unverified payment attempts, amount, method, actual date/time, external reference, recorder, outstanding and refund entitlement.

Use: record only money actually received as a confirmed manual receipt. If a UPI/card payment is not confirmed, keep it pending and do not mark the bill paid. Record partial payments individually. A receipt may allocate to several invoices only through an explicitly implemented allocation flow whose sum equals the receipt.

Manage: changes use reversal/correction events, not silent edits. Deduplicate requests. Apply reviewed cash-receipt limits and aggregation across the correct legal entity, payer/day, transaction and event. The system cannot see receipts kept in another system, so outside receipts need reconciliation. No card PAN, CVV, PIN or banking passwords are stored.

For cash close, expected cash equals opening cash plus confirmed cash receipts and transfers in, less cash refunds, payouts and transfers out. Count actual cash, record a variance with reason/approval, and post the appropriate accounting voucher. Sales totals are not cash totals.

4.11 Customer returns and credit notes

Show: original issued invoice/line, original batch allocations, quantity already returned, remaining entitlement, saved original money/taxes, goods condition, reason and a default Do not restock choice.

Use: owner opens the original bill → Return products → chooses quantities and original batches → records condition/reason → decides disposition → reviews the credit → confirms. A good, eligible return may be explicitly restocked into its original batch only after suitability/resale checks. Cashiers cannot create returns/refunds by default but can reprint approved notes against their own bills.

Manage: partial and repeated returns cumulatively allocate the original amounts exactly; all earlier returns count toward caps, including non-restocked goods. Do not reprice using today's catalogue. A non-restocked return does not inflate sellable stock or automatically reverse inventory/COGS. Keep a separate physical disposition record for goods awaiting quarantine/return/disposal.

A commercial customer credit and an eligible GST liability reduction are separate values/statuses. Preserve original tax amounts as references, but apply a GST adjustment only when its legal conditions and reporting eligibility have been validated. A late/non-qualifying commercial credit can reduce what the customer owes without reducing output GST.

4.12 Refunds, remaining credits and cancellations

Use refunds: approve the refund entitlement → actually return the money outside the software → record date/method/reference/amount → commit once. A refund record does not transfer funds. Pending/failed refunds are not completed cash outflows. Prevent concurrent over-refunds.

Use remaining-invoice credit: after earlier partial returns, credit only the still-uncredited quantities/amounts, including any separately approved service correction. Do not combine an old whole-invoice credit with new partial returns and reverse the same money twice.

Debit notes / undercharge correction: an authorized owner/accountant selects the original issued sale, records the genuine reason, reviews the additional value/tax and issues a separately numbered linked note under the approved legal workflow. A price-only correction changes no stock quantity. Preserve separate source allocations for later credits/returns, including earlier commercial credits; never edit the original amounts or double-credit the adjustment. Additional goods require their own approved fulfillment/document process.

Use cancellation: discard unissued drafts under policy. For an issued invoice, use the authorized legally permitted cancellation/correction process, with reason, timestamps, linked compensating records and any genuine portal acknowledgment. Never delete the issued snapshot or reuse its number. Do not restock goods merely because an invoice status changes; actual custody and suitability matter.

4.13 Accounts and ledgers — explicitly included

Show: customer/supplier, cash/bank/clearing, inventory, sales/services, sales returns/discounts, GST, expense, capital and opening-balance accounts; journals, reversals, trial balance and basic management statements with clearly stated mappings.

Use: post genuine balanced opening vouchers with the accountant; record expenses with supporting references; create balanced journals; review statements and reverse incorrect posted vouchers with linked opposite entries. Period locks control late postings. Automatic sales/purchase/payment/return entries occur in the same transaction as their source action.

Manage: sum of debits equals sum of credits for every posted journal. No invented opening balances or automatic “balancing” revenue. Non-restock returns do not create a false inventory/COGS reversal. Tax-credit eligibility and non-recoverable tax treatment use approved mappings. Basic P&L/balance-sheet outputs are management statements, not audited statutory financial statements. Payroll, depreciation policy, consolidation, income-tax filing and statutory audit remain outside the initial scope unless separately contracted.

4.14 Reports and exports

Preserve these 12 core tabs: Summary, Day book, Products, Categories, Cashiers, Payments, GST, GSTR-1 working register, GSTR-3B working papers, Stock, Receivables and Stock log. Add returns, expiry, purchases/payables and audit/reconciliation views where needed; account statements/trial balance remain under Accounts.

The sales register/drill-down also provides invoice/customer/user detail, cancelled sales, discounts, returns and payment-method totals.

Every report supports approved date presets and custom From/To, branch/registration filters, definitions, drill-down and permission-controlled CSV. GSTR labels must visibly say working papers/registers—not official filed returns or ready-to-file portal JSON. Distinguish gross billed, discounts, commercial credits, eligible GST adjustments, confirmed receipts, refunds, net billed, due and margin.

Historical reports use source event dates and saved snapshots. A receipt today against an older invoice belongs in today's receipts but not today's newly billed sales. Large exports run as durable jobs, remain private and expire according to policy. Quantity-only permissions also apply to downloads and hidden fields.

4.15 Staff, audit, backups and tenant administration

Staff: invite individual users, assign branch/role permissions, require owner/platform MFA, review discount/export/refund rights and disable departed users immediately.

Audit: review actor/time/action, source IDs, safe before/after changes, approvals, exports, permission changes and correction links. The audit trail cannot be turned off by an owner or ordinary application administrator.

Backups: inspect actual last-success time, scope, encryption, India storage evidence where required and restore-test results. An export is not a complete disaster-recovery backup. Tenant recovery must not overwrite other clinics or accidentally import someone else's credentials.

Platform administration: manage tenant activation/entitlements without gaining automatic access to clinic records. Record legal terms/privacy/DPA acceptance versions. Subscription collection is initially manual/externally administered unless a separately approved gateway is built; never pretend the clinic payment ledger is the SaaS provider's subscription ledger.

5. First-time setup checklist

  1. Verify the owner identity and create a tenant through an authenticated onboarding/invitation flow.
  2. Enter the actual business/branch/registration details; review tax status, required records and product/licensing scope.
  3. Complete the compliance-readiness gates and accountant-approved document/tax/ledger configuration.
  4. Configure unique FY series and coordinate them with any other billing system using the same registration.
  5. Create staff accounts and least-privilege permissions; set printing preferences.
  6. Create genuine suppliers/products and reviewed classifications. Import only verified opening stock and balances through controlled tools.
  7. Test the whole workflow using separate staging data, including failed/unknown outcomes, returns and printer widths.
  8. Verify backups, restore, India-required storage/log arrangements and incident contacts before first real bill.
  9. Activate production. Do not enter sample sales or silently reset a tenant to make it look empty.

6. Daily, weekly and month-end management

Opening: confirm the correct tenant/branch, connection/readiness status, receipt printer, active staff and any urgent recalls/expiry alerts. Record genuine drawer opening cash if used.

During the day: receive stock through documents; bill only eligible batches; verify manual digital receipts; resolve unknown outcomes through the operation-status screen rather than posting again under a new key.

Closing: reconcile cash and UPI/card/bank references, review pending receipts/refunds, compare net billed versus cash collected, investigate failed jobs and confirm backup success.

Weekly: perform selective physical stock checks, review near-expiry/quarantine/disposal, outstanding parties, staff access and reconciliation exceptions.

Month/FY end: accountant reviews tax working papers, ITC eligibility, credit/debit-note eligibility, trial balance, inventory valuation and locked periods; approve rule/series changes for future transactions. Legal retention survives tenant closure.

7. Worked counter example

Illustrative only: taxable value ₹1,000 at 12% produces ₹60 CGST + ₹60 SGST in an applicable intra-state case, total ₹1,120. A confirmed ₹700 receipt leaves ₹420 due. A valid 30% customer credit of ₹336 reduces net billed to ₹784 and due to ₹84. A later credit of the remaining ₹784 leaves net billed zero and ₹700 refundable; a confirmed ₹700 refund then clears that entitlement.

The inventory decision is independent: restock only the explicitly eligible original quantities/batches. GST reduction is independently subject to legal eligibility. The invoice remains preserved throughout.

8. Limits users must understand

  • Internet access is required for authoritative billing. No offline sale/payment queue or automatic background financial replay.
  • “Record receipt/refund” is a human-entered record, not bank verification or a transfer.
  • A tax classification lookup is not professional classification approval.
  • An ordinary PDF, hash or payment QR is not an IRN, statutory signature or compliance certificate.
  • A feature excluded from this product may still be a legal obligation of the clinic; use an approved external process or block the affected transaction.
  • Recovery from an old backup is not permission to reuse document numbers or ignore transactions after the recovery point.

4. Tenant isolation and authentication

  1. Authenticate an opaque, high-entropy session cookie. Store only a hash of the session token, with user/membership context and expiry. Use Secure, HttpOnly and an appropriate SameSite policy; enforce CSRF/origin checks for cookie-authenticated mutations.
  2. Resolve the active tenant from a server-validated membership/session selection. A tenant slug, route parameter, body field or subdomain is not authorization.
  3. Load current membership status/permission version and branch/registration scope. Deactivated staff or changed permissions must lose access without waiting for a stale UI cache.
  4. Build a trusted context { userId, tenantId, membershipId, allowedBranchIds, permissions, requestId }. Pass it explicitly through controllers, domain services, repositories, jobs and file access.
  5. Every query/update/delete/aggregation/import/export lookup uses the tenant predicate; every related entity must match tenant and appropriate branch/registration. Lookup pipelines must scope both sides.

6. Exact money, quantities and tax

  • Keep report aggregation exact too: guard MongoDB Long-overflow promotion to double, use validated Decimal128 intermediate aggregation or bounded BigInt reductions, and return paise strings without unsafe coercion.
  • Parse decimal strings; use BigInt for calculations. Store paise and scaled quantity units using BSON Long, with explicit bounds. JSON transports them as strings. Do not call Number()/parseFloat() on money or enable driver options that silently promote large values to unsafe JS numbers.
  • Define each UOM's scale and legal unit/pack conversion. Prefer explicit integer ratios. A fractional strip/tablet conversion is not authorized dispensing merely because arithmetic supports it.
  • Represent rates as exact rational/scaled values with defined precision; do not assume only a small hardcoded list of integer-percentage slabs.
  • Quote and finalize through one backend calculator. The browser uses returned amounts; PDF/report generators use saved snapshots, not today's tax functions.
  • Define an approved rounding policy for components, lines, invoice discount allocation and final invoice adjustment. Deterministic largest-remainder/cumulative allocation must reconcile to original totals.
  • For new applicable intra-state transactions with matching component rates, calculate one half-component under the approved policy and use the equal amount for CGST and SGST/UTGST. Never split an odd combined paise by putting one extra paise into only one component.
  • Tax-inclusive fixed prices and equal paise components can produce mathematically incompatible rounding cases. Use only a reviewed explicit taxable/rounding/price policy; otherwise reject the quote and require correction before issue. Never disguise an unexplained difference or alter history to force equality.
  • Preserve legacy snapshots, including historical unequal components. Returns/corrections of those documents retain their actual original basis; do not fabricate different historical taxes.

Example boundary helper (real implementation needs validation and tests):

import { Long } from 'mongodb';

export function toStoredPaise(value) {
  if (typeof value !== 'string' || !/^-?\d+$/.test(value)) {
    throw new Error('Integer paise string required');
  }
  const n = BigInt(value);
  if (n < -(2n ** 63n) || n > 2n ** 63n - 1n) {
    throw new Error('Money outside storage bounds');
  }
  return Long.fromBigInt(n);
}

The UI may use small ordinary numbers for page sizes and dates; the prohibition is against unsafe monetary/quantity arithmetic, not all JavaScript numbers.

7. Atomic billing and idempotency

Every financial mutation requires a random operation key scoped to tenant, actor and action, plus a canonical validated payload hash. Retrying the same operation returns its original result; the same identity with a different payload is rejected. Keep a durable minimal deduplication record for the replay-risk/legal retention period even if optional cached response data expires.

For ordinary supported sales without an external statutory prerequisite:

  1. Authenticate/authorize and validate the request size, identifiers and draft version.
  2. In a real Mongo transaction, check the idempotency identity and matching payload.
  3. Resolve same-tenant products/parties/branches/registration, approved effective rules and legal prerequisites. Revalidate price/discount authority, expiry and stock.
  4. Recalculate using the backend calculator and compare the approved quote/draft revision. Do not silently charge a changed amount.
  5. Select FEFO eligible stock layers. Use conditional version/available-quantity updates in the transaction. A failed condition causes a clean conflict, not negative stock.
  6. Reserve the final number with an atomic counter in its legal scope; no count-plus-one or frontend numbering.
  7. Insert immutable invoice/line snapshots, original stock/cost allocations, inventory movements, balanced GL postings, any genuine confirmed receipt, audit event and outbox jobs.
  8. Write the durable idempotency result in that transaction. Commit with appropriate majority durability; only then return “issued”.

8. Returns, credits, refunds and cancellations

Original line/batch allocations must contain the exact sold quantity, discount, taxable value, tax components, gross and cost basis. For an ordinary unadjusted original sale, allocate partial returns cumulatively:

new amount = rounded original amount × cumulative returned quantity / original quantity − amount already credited

Use a deterministic approved algorithm for every component, with the last eligible return absorbing only legitimate residuals so full cumulative returns exactly equal the original. Do not recalculate from a current price/rate. Later authorized debit/price-correction notes must remain separate immutable source-component entitlement buckets with explicit original-line references. Design and test their allocation, and the effect of earlier non-quantity commercial credits, under the approved policy; the simple unadjusted formula is not a universal algorithm for those cases. Preserve physical quantity caps and avoid crediting any source component twice. A transaction must conditionally consume the remaining return entitlement and, if selected, restore eligible original stock. Concurrent returns cannot both spend the same entitlement.

  • Default restock is false. Require explicit permission, condition/suitability confirmation and eligible original batch/location before restoring stock.
  • No-restock returns do not add saleable quantity or automatically restore inventory/COGS. Record actual physical custody/disposition separately, with zero restored book cost unless an approved explicit valuation/reclassification process applies. Do not later write off that same original cost a second time.
  • Commercial credits and output-GST adjustments have separate eligibility/amounts. Preserve original tax reference fields even where permitted GST adjustment is zero.
  • A credit note changes the customer's obligation, not the bank account. A refund is a distinct confirmed money record with method/reference/date/actor, permission and idempotency.
  • Remaining/full credits consider all previous partial credits and delivered-service constraints. Never reverse an entire invoice twice.
  • Cancellation appends legally appropriate events/compensating documents. Draft deletion, issued cancellation, tax credit, goods return and refund are not interchangeable. Keep the original number/snapshot.

Base-case settlement definition, before optional advances/external-credit transfers:

net billed = original issued gross + valid debit adjustments − customer credits
net money received = confirmed receipts − confirmed refunds − confirmed receipt reversals
due = max(net billed − net money received, 0)
refund entitlement = max(net money received − net billed, 0)

For broader allocation features, model explicit credit applications and their double-entry effects; do not simply subtract a credit twice. Pending payments/refunds have no completed cash effect. Block unexplained overpayments; advances need an approved voucher/time-of-supply flow before activation.

12. Durable jobs, files and serverless constraints

Outbox insertion occurs in the same DB transaction as its source action. Jobs have tenant/source/purpose, unique event key, payload version/reference, state, attempts, next attempt time, lease token/expiry and result. Cron claims due work atomically, processes small bounded chunks, and checkpoints progress. Only the current lease token may commit results; a crashed worker's expired lease can be reclaimed safely.

Handlers must be idempotent; assume at-least-once invocation, overlap and partial failure. Use bounded backoff, dead-letter/manual-redrive review and provider-specific status reconciliation. “Exactly once” is achieved for a financial database event using transactions/unique identities, not promised for every external email. No in-memory job list, local persistent backup, startup timer or unawaited process controls core correctness.

Large exports use consistent defined snapshots/watermarks, pagination and private object storage. Do not put large exports or backup archives through a normal buffered API response. Vercel imposes function/body/execution limits that vary by runtime/plan; verify current limits and keep normal bodies conservatively below them. 1

Private file metadata includes tenant, object key, uploader, purpose, allowed readers, content type/hash, size, retention and scan state. Server generates object keys and scoped upload permissions. Never trust a client-provided tenant prefix or public URL. Validate type/size and scan external documents before use; prevent SSRF. Short-lived download capabilities must be minted only after authorization; avoid PII/secrets in paths and logs. 2. Core GST/document controls

LEG-01 — Supplier status and correct document

Configure regular GST registration, composition status and unregistered businesses as distinct profiles. Verify the actual GSTIN/legal identity/status and date range; syntactic checksum validity alone is not official registration verification. An unregistered person must not collect GST as if registered. Composition and exempt-supply document logic must be reviewed; do not label every printout a GST tax invoice.

Support tax invoice, bill of supply, appropriate ordinary commercial sale record, credit/debit notes, receipts and cancellation/return records. Where legally applicable, invoice-cum-bill-of-supply is a distinct reviewed capability, not a generic mixed-cart shortcut. Required fields depend on document and transaction facts. Rule 49 specifies bill-of-supply particulars. 1

Release evidence: reviewed document eligibility/field matrix, genuine tenant identity approval, blocked invalid combinations, templates tested on A4 and thermal widths. Preserve electronic-invoice signature exceptions only where actually applicable; do not issue a blanket “no signature ever required” statement.

LEG-02 — Invoice numbering and particulars

Rule 46 includes a consecutive serial number, no more than 16 characters, permitted letters/numbers/hyphen/slash, unique for a financial year, plus transaction-dependent supplier/recipient, description, quantity/UQC, tax, place-of-supply and other particulars. Recipient particulars at specified values and requested particulars below those values must follow the current rule. 2

Build: server-reserved series per legal registration/FY, full-number uniqueness, no reuse after cancellation/reservation, FY rollover tests and a number register. All prefixes/separators count toward length. Coordinate series with external billing channels. A branch ID is not a substitute for correct registration-wide uniqueness.

Release evidence: concurrency tests, legacy/external number migration, cancelled-number history, recipient-field boundary fixtures and required declarations reviewed by the CA.

LEG-03 — HSN/SAC and rates

No product is legally classified merely because a suggested code matches a regex. Notification 78/2020 specifies 4/6-digit HSN requirements by preceding-year turnover, with the stated relief for qualifying supplies to unregistered recipients; portal validations and later instruments also need review. 1

Build: reviewed classifications and effective-dated rate/treatment versions with source/evidence. A reference catalogue is not a catalogue of approved tenant rates. Do not seed a guessed rate for every medicine or freeze an obsolete list of GST slabs. Validate current notifications for the actual product and supply date.

Release evidence: product-by-product approval or approved classification policy; tests for missing/invalid codes, changed rates, exemption versus nil rating and immutable historic snapshots.

LEG-04 — Veterinary healthcare is not the entire pet business

Entry 46 of Notification 12/2017–Central Tax (Rate), under heading 9983, covers services by a veterinary clinic in relation to healthcare of animals or birds. It is not a blanket exemption for all medicines, pet food, retail supplies, grooming, boarding or software subscriptions. The base notification and current amendments/applicability must be checked for the specific supply. 1

Build: independent, approved goods/service treatments; no automatic healthcare exemption for the whole cart. Composite/mixed supplies, ancillary dispensing and bundled packages require professional classification before activation. The SaaS provider's own subscription supply is separate from the clinic's veterinary supplies.

LEG-05 — Place of supply and tax components

The IGST Act's goods rules distinguish movement, bill-to/ship-to and other cases, including section 10(1)(ca) for unregistered recipients and recorded addresses. 1 Circular 209/03/2024 addresses the specified unregistered-recipient delivery/billing-address issue. 1

Build: a reviewed place-of-supply resolver accepting supplier registration, goods/service type, recipient status, delivery/movement and special-case facts. Service rules require separate review; a customer's home state alone is insufficient. Use SGST or UTGST as legally applicable. For supported new ordinary intra-state supplies with equal component rates, preserve the project's equal CGST/SGST-or-UTGST requirement using an approved rounding policy. Never rewrite historical unequal saved tax components.

Gate: exports, SEZ, reverse charge, unusual bundles, distinct-person stock transfers and other unsupported cases remain blocked until scoped and validated.

LEG-06 — Value, discounts and rounding

Section 15 governs value and discount treatment. The CBIC active text accessed in this research includes Finance Act 2026 changes marked “w.e.f. yet to be notified”; a coding agent must not apply bracketed future text merely because it appears on the page. Verify the operative pre-amendment text and any subsequent commencement notification at implementation/release. 1

Build: one backend exact-arithmetic tax service; explicit before-/at-/post-supply discount eligibility, invoice-discount allocation and inclusive-price residual policy. Do not hide rounding differences by altering an issued snapshot. Distinguish paise-level calculation policy from legally prescribed rounding of statutory amounts. Review the actual rounding/time-of-supply treatment; illustrative rates are not product advice.

LEG-07 — Customer credits are not automatically GST reductions

Section 34 provides for credit/debit notes. Its current text specifies the credit-note declaration cut-off of 30 November following the supply FY or furnishing of the relevant annual return, whichever is earlier, plus conditions on reducing output tax liability. The accessed text also shows a proviso effective 1 October 2025 concerning attributable ITC reversal by a registered recipient who availed it, and other incidence-of-tax cases. A further 2026 insertion is marked awaiting commencement. 1

Build: separate original tax reference, customer commercial-credit value, eligible GST adjustment, eligibility review and reporting status. Do not misdescribe a reporting/declaration deadline as a universal prohibition on making any later commercial credit. Record the real annual-return filing date when relevant. Refund recording never substitutes for a required note or proves GST reduction.

Gate: review late notes, post-sale discounts, recipient ITC evidence and supplier-return legal document roles. A buyer's purchase-return memo is not automatically a supplier-issued statutory debit note.

LEG-08 — E-invoice applicability and authentic IRN

Notification 10/2023 reduces the relevant e-invoicing turnover figure to five crore rupees from 1 August 2023; the applicable parent notification, turnover look-back, supplier exemptions and document/recipient coverage must also be assessed. The official listing describes taxpayers exceeding ₹5 crore; preserve the operative wording, not a loose threshold label. 1

A separate GSTN advisory applies a 30-day reporting restriction to covered IRN documents for AATO ₹10 crore and above from 1 April 2025, including relevant invoices, credit notes and debit notes. This is not the general applicability threshold. 5

Build: record verified applicability per registration, authentic IRN/signed payload/QR and cancellation acknowledgments only through approved integration or a properly verified external workflow. A normal PDF or UPI QR is not e-invoicing. Do not deliver a legally ineffective “final invoice” where IRN is mandatory but missing. If this integration remains outside initial scope, restrict the product to eligible supported transactions/tenants rather than waive their obligation.

LEG-09 — New/proposed portal features are not automatically operative law

GSTN's October 2026 advisory on recipient-generated RCM self-invoices describes RCMDOM/RCMIMP as available for sandbox work, with production rollout, applicability and commencement governed by further notifications/communication. 2

Build: track this as a change-watch item, not as an enabled production obligation based only on a recommendation/sandbox feature. More generally, recheck current notifications after Council meetings before deploying tax changes. Do not fabricate self-invoice IRNs.

LEG-10 — E-way bills, dynamic B2C QR and special supplies

Rule 138 contains conditions for e-way-bill information and consignment-value calculation, with exceptions and state-specific implications. Do not implement a single “every bill over ₹50,000 needs an e-way bill” rule. 5

Gate: determine goods movement, transporter/document facts, state rules, exemptions, current validity/cancellation/portal requirements and any separately applicable dynamic B2C QR obligation. These integrations remain excluded unless approved; block affected dispatches or use a reviewed external process. RCM/advances, cess, exports/SEZ and interstate stock transfers are not silently treated as ordinary domestic retail.

LEG-11 — ITC and inventory losses

Input tax eligibility is subject to the relevant conditions, exclusions, evidence and reversals; a purchase invoice or an equal split of a combined tax figure does not establish claimable ITC. 7

Build: pending/eligible/ineligible review; actual supplier tax components; accountant-approved tax/expense/inventory reclassification. Review mixed exempt/taxable activity, losses/write-offs, gifts and other blocked/reversal cases under the applicable provisions. GSTR-2B matching and return filing are not invented by the application. Preserve source facts even when a professional review later changes eligibility.

3. Books, electronic records and cash

LEG-12 — GST stock accounts, audit trail and authentication

Rule 56 covers stock/accounts, document registers, advances and relevant supply records; it requires electronic edit/delete logs and includes authentication of electronic records by digital signature. Its requirements are not satisfied merely by creating a pretty audit screen. 1

Build: append-only financial documents/movements/journals, immutable change events, role-controlled read/export, reproducible record packages and a professionally reviewed record-authentication/signing workflow where applicable. A SHA-256 hash, scanned signature or software checkbox is not a statutory digital signature. Do not confuse an invoice signature exception under rule 46 with the separate rules for electronic books.

LEG-13 — GST retention and recoverability

Section 36 generally requires relevant GST records for 72 months from the due date of the annual return for the relevant year; specified proceedings/investigations can extend retention to one year after final disposal or that period, whichever is later. 3 Rule 57 requires proper electronic backup and production of readable/authenticated records on demand. 1

Build: record-class retention calculation, legal holds, readable exports, restore tests and preserved financial snapshots. Do not implement “delete all tenant data after 30 days” or a blanket “six years from invoice date” policy. Privacy erasure must coexist with required lawful retention.

LEG-14 — Current income-tax books and India backups

The notified Income-tax Rules, 2026 commence on 1 April 2026. Rule 46(8) addresses the specified electronic books/documents remaining accessible in India and daily backups on servers physically in India. Rule 46(9) prescribes seven tax years from the end of the relevant tax year for the specified records, with the further reopened-assessment provision. Applicability is tied to the persons/books described in the rule, not every database merely because it is Indian. 1

The primary PDF was retrieved and rule 46 read on PDF pages 37–38. Rule 46(6) also refers to additional medical-profession records, including a daily case register in Form No. 25 and specified stock inventories. Whether and how these apply to the client's veterinary activity requires CA review. Do not casually substitute an old form number or claim a billing-only product fulfills clinical/professional recordkeeping.

Build: actual India backup evidence, daily-success monitoring, the applicable retention clock and external-record workflow where a required professional register is outside scope. Assess transitional periods under the old/new income-tax legislation with the CA.

LEG-15 — Company books and non-disableable accounting audit trail

For company tenants, section 128 includes double-entry/accrual books and at least eight preceding financial years of relevant books/vouchers, subject to applicable extensions. 2 ICAI's revised 2024 implementation guide addresses the company accounting-software audit-trail requirement effective for the relevant FYs from 1 April 2023, including operation/preservation and tampering considerations. 13

The MCA primary Fourth Amendment Rules, 2022 were also retrieved: they amend access to electronic books to “at all times”, replace periodic with daily backup, and add particulars of a person in India where the provider is outside India. Primary URL: https://www.mca.gov.in/bin/ebook/dms/getdocument?doc=MTcyODIyOTI0&docCategory=Notifications&type=open

Build: no disable switch for accounting audit trails; complete transaction/change history and India-accessible/daily backup arrangements where applicable. Application owners and runtime database credentials must not have routine alteration/deletion rights over immutable financial/audit collections. Exceptional privileged maintenance must be controlled and independently evidenced. Hash chains are tamper-evidence, not proof that a privileged database administrator cannot tamper.

Verification limit: the legacy MCA 2021 PDF URL returned an error page during research. The accounting audit-trail conclusion here uses the official professional-body guide; the current amended primary rule/notifications must still be reviewed before company-tenant release.

LEG-16 — Cash receipt restrictions

The official Income-tax Department text of section 186, Income-tax Act 2025, prohibits receipt of ₹2,00,000 or more in the specified non-permitted modes under the alternative person/day, single-transaction and event/occasion tests, subject to stated exemptions. It is not just a per-invoice limit. 1

Build: approved payment-mode and aggregation rules; entity-wide checks across branches, payer identity where required, split-payment detection, recorded exception evidence and conservative controls when outside receipts are unknown. Do not encourage splitting bills, changing customers or recording cash as UPI to avoid a limit. Review other applicable cash/PAN/reporting requirements and the current rules, including prescribed electronic modes, with the CA. A warning cannot make a prohibited receipt lawful.

LEG-17 — Drug licences, prescriptions and restricted categories

Do not equate every clinic with a licensed retail pharmacy or assume an own-patient exemption authorizes an open shop. LEG-18 — MRP and drug price control

Do not add GST over an already tax-inclusive MRP or disguise an overcharge as a product fee. Validate lawful split-pack dispensing separately.

LEG-19 — Expiry, recall, quarantine and disposal

Financial write-off is not evidence of lawful physical disposal. Capture the actual approved disposal method/vendor/documentation; do not generate fake disposal certificates or instruct staff to discard medicine into ordinary waste. No-restock customer returns must not restore sellable quantity or create duplicate cost reversals.

5. Financial workflows and transaction implementation

5.1 Quote and finalize

Drafts are versioned, not issued invoices. A quote is computed on the backend from same-tenant references and applicable rule versions. Revalidate all facts at finalization: stock/expiry/status, price/discount authority, supply/recipient/PoS, mandatory fields, licensing/e-invoice prerequisites and draft/quote version. Do not silently accept a changed total; return a reviewable conflict.

For a supported ordinary sale, one Mongo transaction must:

  1. Resolve the operation identity and matching canonical payload hash.
  2. Validate same-tenant references and legal readiness; recalculate exact approved totals.
  3. Allocate eligible FEFO stock and perform conditional available-quantity/version updates. No count-then-write race, negative stock or expired-batch override.
  4. Atomically obtain the legal registration/FY series number; no frontend or count-plus-one generation.
  5. Insert immutable complete financial/line snapshots and original batch/cost allocations.
  6. Insert movements, balanced source-unique GL entries, any actual confirmed receipt, audit and unique outbox events.
  7. Record the durable idempotency result; commit majority-durable and only then respond issued.

Every repository operation uses the same session. Do not use Promise.all or network/PDF/email/storage side effects inside a retriable callback. Do not send an HTTP response inside it. Bound transaction/driver retries to the function budget. Handle the specifically expected idempotency conflict, not every duplicate-key error, as a possible replay. Driver guidance: 1

Keep finalization/source-draft uniqueness so a new key cannot finalize an already issued draft twice. Operation status lookup remains authorized. If a response is lost, preserve the original key and resolve it; no new financial queue/retry on reconnect.

5.2 Numbering and state

Reserve/generate server-side; enforce full printed-number format/length, approved scopes and FY behavior. An unexposed candidate rolled back with its whole transaction is not an issued or durably reserved number. Separate immutable document truth from mutable state projections; business lifecycle, statutory acceptance/cancellation, settlement and physical fulfillment are distinct dimensions.

5.3 Purchases, inventory and adjustments

Posting a genuine receipt creates stock/cost layers, movements/payable/tax-review/accounting once. Direct product quantity editing is not an opening/receipt/adjustment workflow. Document actual supplier tax; never split a single total into invented input components or auto-claim ITC. Partial/bonus receipts and supplier returns require exact quantity/cost policy and source links. If a partial receipt/accrual flow is not implemented, block it instead of inventing a full receipt.

Month/year expiry retains its precision and reviewed sell-by cutoff; recalled/quarantined/cold-chain-unverified/damaged stock is ineligible even if a calendar expiry is future. Required unknown expiry blocks sale. Backdating a new counter sale must not bypass current custody, expiry or period controls. Disposal records actual approved handling, not merely a UI balance subtraction. Support authorized internal consumption, wastage and damage/expiry write-offs as explicit movement/voucher types with approved expense/COGS/tax-review treatment, not hidden quantity edits.

5.4 Returns and credit entitlement

Use the original finalized invoice, line and sold batch allocations. All previous partial, full, commercial and no-restock credits consume the correct entitlement. For the ordinary unadjusted sale, cumulative monetary allocation must reconcile exactly:

new component = rounded(original component × cumulative returned quantity / original quantity) − previously allocated component

Use an approved deterministic implementation with proper caps/residuals. Current product prices/rates cannot affect old return amounts. The last valid return must reconcile original quantities, discounts, taxable value, components and gross—not create an extra credit from rounding.

Authorized later debit/price-correction notes are separate immutable source-component entitlement buckets linked to the original line. Explicitly design/test their return allocation and the effect of earlier non-quantity commercial credits; the simple unadjusted formula is not universally sufficient. Never edit originals, over-return physical quantity or credit a source component twice. Provide a reviewed undercharge/debit-note workflow with correct numbering/tax/GL and no stock movement for a price-only change.

Restock defaults false. Only explicitly approved suitable goods in an eligible original batch/location can increase saleable stock and reverse the appropriate original allocated cost/COGS. A non-restocked return creates no false inventory/COGS reversal; record physical returned/quarantine/disposal custody separately and avoid a second cost write-off. Refuse ordinary product-return credits for an already delivered service unless the separate authorized correction workflow permits it.

Commercial customer credit and eligible GST adjustment are separate. Keep original tax reference versus actual GST adjustment/status/evidence; late or ineligible commercial credits can change customer balance without lowering output GST. Remaining/full credit considers earlier returns and cannot reverse an invoice twice. Concurrent returns conditionally consume remaining entitlement in the same transaction.

5.5 Payments, refunds and cancellation

Confirmed manual receipts mean staff actually verified money received; pending/unverified UPI/card is not paid. Record method/reference/date/amount/actor and exact allocations. No card PAN/CVV/PIN/bank password. Duplicate reference checks follow the actual provider/account semantics; split allocations do not become duplicate receipts.

A credit does not send money. A refund is a separate permissioned, idempotent confirmed outflow after actual external payment, capped transactionally by real entitlement. Pending/failed refunds do not reduce cash. Support receipt reversals/corrections with history; block unexplained overpayments/advances until an approved receipt-voucher/time-of-supply flow exists.

Base supported-case settlement: net billed = original gross + valid debit adjustments − customer credits; net received = confirmed receipts − confirmed refunds − confirmed receipt reversals; due/refund entitlement are positive differences in the appropriate direction. Explicit credit transfers/advances require separate allocation/accounting design, never double subtraction.

Draft discard is not issued cancellation. Issued correction/cancellation needs permission/reason, preserved number/core, lawful eligibility, genuine external acknowledgment if required, linked financial/stock events and separate refund. Cancellation cannot magically return goods to custody or erase bank movements.
