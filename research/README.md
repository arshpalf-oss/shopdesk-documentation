# Research evidence — limits and interpretation

Review cut-off: 26 October 2026. These are public-source research excerpts and retrieval metadata, not professional legal approval or an exhaustive current-law consolidation.

- `primary-source-retrieval.json` records actual PDF bytes, hashes, page counts, scan limits and failures. Initial marker scans were selective; subsequent reads are separately recorded.
- `income-rules-2026-rule46.txt` contains the complete relevant rule on PDF pages 37–38 and the adjacent page.
- `drugs-rules-excerpts.txt` was regenerated after a normalized scan, including PDF pages 42, 43 and 46. Its official compilation heading is dated 2022; current central/state amendments remain a release check.
- DPDP Rules, CERT-In directions and selected income/drugs provisions are excerpted for the stated engineering questions, not reproduced as an authoritative complete legal database.
- Full downloaded PDFs are not bundled. Their official URLs and retrieved hashes allow a reviewer to retrieve the source. Public sources can change or be blocked later.
- The consolidated Income-tax Act URL returned 403. The direct eGazette PDF attempt failed certificate verification; TLS verification was not bypassed. The specific official 2026 section 186 HTML page was subsequently retrieved and read.
- MeitY's December 2025 corrigenda and NPPA's June 2026 DPCO amendment were read through the page-fetch tool. These are included in the source register; not every other current amendment was verified.
- The legal matrix records remaining source, effective-date, professional, licence, privacy and record-authentication questions. A URL, hash, research excerpt or successful document-structure check is not software/legal certification.

The internal extraction helper is not part of the packaged deliverables. No research script is an application component, migration or production command.
